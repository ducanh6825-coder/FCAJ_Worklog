## Weekly achievements

- Distinguish buckets, objects, keys, and prefixes and explain S3 object storage versus EBS block storage.
- Understand Block Public Access, Object Ownership, and encryption as document protection controls.
- Distinguish bucket-level ListBucket from object-level GetObject/PutObject permissions and scope access by prefix.
- Verify downloaded files by comparing contents or hashes against the originals.
- Understand gateway endpoint routes and why successful S3 access alone does not prove endpoint usage.
