## Weekly achievements

- Understand metrics, namespaces, and dimensions and select the correct Instance ID for CPUUtilization.
- Distinguish statistics and periods and explain their effects on CPU graph interpretation.
- Understand OK, ALARM, and INSUFFICIENT_DATA states and missing-data handling.
- Distinguish log groups and streams; understand retention and the Agent role in collecting server logs.
- Correlate event timestamps with application actions and exclude passwords, cookies, and credentials from logs.
