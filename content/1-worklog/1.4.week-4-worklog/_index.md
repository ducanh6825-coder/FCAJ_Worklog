## Weekly achievements

- Distinguish stateful resource-level Security Groups from stateless subnet-level Network ACLs.
- Understand Security Group allow rules and Network ACL allow/deny rules evaluated in ascending rule-number order.
- Restrict SSH to the administrator IP and identify the HTTPS port required by the website.
- Understand why NACLs must allow outbound and response traffic, including appropriate ephemeral ports.
- Design allow/deny tests and preserve original settings for rollback.
