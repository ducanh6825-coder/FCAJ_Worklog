- Understand how login, listing, uploads, and downloads rely on session validation.
- Separate secrets from source code and use an IAM role to access a private bucket.
- Check S3 permission scope and distinguish authorization failures from transport problems.
- Understand HTTPS configuration and checks for cookies, logout, and session expiration during integration.
