## Weekly achievements

- Determine IPv4/CIDR ranges and understand that subnets must fit within the VPC without overlapping.
- Distinguish private/public addresses and the roles of VPCs, subnets, and Availability Zones.
- Understand routing decisions and how an Internet Gateway supports eligible resources connecting to the Internet.
- Explain why an Internet route alone is insufficient: appropriate addressing, firewall rules, and a listening service are also needed.
- Use names and the Project tag to identify resources and check CIDR ranges before deployment.
