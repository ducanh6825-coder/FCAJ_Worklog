- Distinguish AMIs, instance types, and EBS and understand stopping versus terminating an instance.

- Understand SSH key authentication and distinguish key errors from connectivity issues.
- Use Linux commands for files, permissions, packages, and services.
- Check service status with systemctl and use journalctl to investigate startup errors.
